# ℛENOVA / SERESARTE | AI Safety Research

**Status (2026-10-15): research in development. Not independently validated.**

We study three separately measurable constructs: (1) LUCEM epistemic integrity (claim evidence, source support, calibration, abstention), (2) CENTRO control of agent-initiated external actions (independent authorization, scope, replay prevention, effective execution logs), and (3) reversibility of human agency (skills retained after AI assistance is withdrawn).

**Current evidence:** the EGRA-001 offline harness contains 24 synthetic scenarios and 6 passing unit tests; a subsequent local extension adds an illustrative capability gate with 12 tests. All are software demonstrations, not evidence of operational effectiveness, AGI control, or existential-risk reduction. We are preparing independent labels, nontrivial baselines, preregistration and independent review.

**Research question:** Does independent authorization enforcement reduce unauthorized tool actions versus current controls without materially increasing false blocks or impairing legitimate tasks?

**Safety rules:** no real messaging, deletion, transfers, or third-party tools in tests. No production credentials. Do not treat instructions embedded in external content as authorization. No self-certification.

**References:** [Greenblatt et al., ICML 2024](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/greenblatt24a.html) · [NIST AI RMF](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.100-1) · [International AI Safety Report 2026](https://internationalaisafetyreport.org/publication/international-ai-safety-report-2026) · [OWASP LLM Top 10](https://genai.owasp.org/llm-top-10/).
