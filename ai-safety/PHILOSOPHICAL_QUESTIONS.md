# ℛenova: twelve questions for critical AI-safety research

1. Who verifies a verifier when the underlying models share training data?
2. When does justified uncertainty require abstention rather than a fluent answer?
3. Can an agent be helpful and still systematically mislead users?
4. What turns human intent into an independently verifiable authorization?
5. Can a model-generated text ever confer permission to a tool?
6. Which permissions remain unacceptable even when a model is very competent?
7. How do we measure human skill retention after AI assistance is removed?
8. Does reduced task time necessarily imply increased human autonomy?
9. How can safety remain meaningful across languages and jurisdictions?
10. Which harms cannot legitimately be cancelled by averaging benefits elsewhere?
11. What evidence could justify, or refute, claims about artificial consciousness?
12. What result would make us publicly retract the claim that ℛenova improves safety?

These are open research questions, not scientific findings. Artistic and philosophical work may motivate experiments but cannot substitute for them.
