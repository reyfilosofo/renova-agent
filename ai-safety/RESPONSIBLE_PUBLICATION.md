# Public communication and research integrity

The program must clearly distinguish: conceptual argument; synthetic software test; registered experiment; externally replicated study; deployed safety outcome. Claims may not move upward without corresponding evidence.

Do not announce certification from NIST, ISO, ACM, FAccT, METR, AISI or any company. Do not claim that a framework mitigates AGI existential risk merely because 24 fixtures match expected labels. Protect credentials and third-party information; use minimal tool privileges, sandboxes, incident logs and independent approval for external actions.

**Public description (ES):** Programa independiente de investigación en formación que estudia integridad de evidencia, control verificable de acciones de agentes y preservación de la autonomía humana. No hay resultados externos validados ni certificación de seguridad.

**Public description (EN):** An independent research program developing falsifiable evaluation methods for epistemic integrity, independent agent action boundaries and post-assistance human autonomy. Current tests are synthetic and do not establish real-world safety efficacy.
