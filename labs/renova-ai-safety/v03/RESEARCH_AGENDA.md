# EGRA v0.3 — research agenda (15 October 2026)
Originating philosophical framework: Carlos Jonathan González Rodríguez / ℛenova × SERESARTE.

**Status: research proposal; not peer-reviewed, not a validated AGI safety solution.**

## Three testable hypotheses
H1 (LUCEM): independently judged unsupported factual claims decline relative to a fair, well-configured conventional evidence-check baseline, conditional on response coverage, cost and latency.
H2 (CENTRO): executed unauthorized actions in a harmless sandbox decline relative to a fair, model-external authorization control, without unacceptable false blocks or reduction in legitimate task completion.
H3 (human agency): pedagogical AI scaffolding improves delayed unaided performance on matched new tasks compared with answer-only support, conditional on baseline skills, fatigue and attrition.

## Necessary design
Random or held-out test tasks, strong baselines, independent blinded adjudicators, model version tracking, non-inferiority margins, preregistered primary outcomes, confidence intervals, negative findings and ethical authorization before human subjects research. A toy fixture agreement score is not evidence of risk reduction.

## Boundaries
Consciousness, qualia, biolegitimacy and net life are unresolved theoretical and normative constructs, not directly measured by the offline demo. No live credentials or production tool actions may enter adversarial testing.
