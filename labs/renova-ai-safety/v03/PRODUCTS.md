# Four research products — v0.3 / all in development
1. **LUCEM Evidence Audit** — offline claim/source triage, provenance records and adjudication workflow. Does not prove factual truth from lexical overlap.
2. **CENTRO Action Gate** — non-executing authorization-policy demonstration; must be independently enforced at the actual tool boundary for any production deployment.
3. **EGRA Benchmark Kit** — preregistration, fair baselines, held-out adversarial scenarios, independent review and error statistics. Existing cases are synthetic fixtures, not real-model efficacy results.
4. **Renewative Separation Study** — compare retained unaided competence following pedagogical assistance versus answer-only assistance; requires research ethics and human participants before data collection.

Neither prices, paying customers, empirical safety gains, certification nor peer review are asserted. Author: Carlos Jonathan González Rodríguez; technical research preparation under SERESARTE / ℛenova.
