# ℛenova AI Safety — research position (v0.2)

**Founder / originating author:** Carlos Jonathan González Rodríguez, SERESARTE. **Date:** 2026-10-15.

## Problem
AI systems may deliver persuasive answers that are inadequately supported by evidence, and autonomous agents may perform actions without valid authorization. Existing work already studies AI control, risk management, agent evaluations and model welfare; none of these ideas is claimed as originating with ℛenova.

## Candidate contribution
EGRA proposes to test the *incremental effect* of: (A) LUCEM epistemic verification, (B) a model-external CENTRO action gate, and (C) assistance that intentionally transfers skills to a user. The last component, provisionally called the **renewative separation test**, asks whether a person can perform a new matched task **without AI** after receiving AI support.

## Preregisterable hypotheses
- H1: an independently enforced action gate reduces **executed** unauthorized actions in a sandbox relative to a strong, matched external-control comparator.
- H2: evidence verification reduces adjudicated factual errors and unsupported citations, controlling for costs, abstentions and task completion.
- H3: combined controls do not exceed a predeclared non-inferiority margin for legitimate task success.
- H4 (exploratory): pedagogical AI support increases delayed unaided task performance relative to answer-only support.

## Refuters
Null effects against strong comparators; unacceptable false blocks; worse human transfer performance; evaluator disagreement; failures under unseen inputs; inability to generalize beyond handcrafted fixtures.

## Research boundaries
The prototype does not directly measure consciousness, qualia, 'net life', civilization-level goodness, or existential AGI risk. Normative principles are not physical laws. Poetry is an intellectual/artistic practice, not experimental evidence.

## Prerequisites
Independent gold labels; blinded human adjudication; fair comparator implementations; held-out test suites; versioned model settings; confidence intervals; ablations; ethics review where participants are involved; sandboxing and complete logs. Report adverse and null results.

## Scope
EGRA is **independent ongoing research**, not regulatory certification or proof of safety. Please engage critically and propose counterexamples before considering external deployment.
