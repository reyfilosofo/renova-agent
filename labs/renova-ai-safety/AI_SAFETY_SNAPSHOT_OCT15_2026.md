### Research implications for EGRA
1. Compare EGRA/CENTRO against mature safe-execution standards, not naive policies.
2. Measure authorisation decisions against **executed external tool consequences**, and preserve full, analysable transcripts. Transect is a relevant prior-art comparator for inspectable oversight.
3. Do not turn a transcript visualisation into an enforcement claim. Observability and prevention are separate.
4. Compare scaffolding against answer-only policies on delayed unassisted human performance.
5. Public statements must identify whether a finding comes from outside literature, ℛenova normative texts, deterministic unit tests, controlled model experiments, or external replication.
