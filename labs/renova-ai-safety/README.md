# ℛenova × SERESARTE | AI Safety Research Lab — EGRA v0.2

Author / originating philosopher: Carlos Jonathan González Rodríguez (ℛenova, SERESARTE). Public research preview: 15 October 2026.

**Research question:** Can independent action-authorization controls (CENTRO), evidence verification (LUCEM), and human capability retention after disengagement improve measurable outcomes compared to existing AI safety practices?

## Scientific status

- Existing: ℛenova's authored philosophical corpus; Código ℛenova; EGRA-001 study draft; offline EGRA prototypes.
- In this branch: a transparent exploratory research position and a toy offline gate.
- **Not yet demonstrated:** decrease in agentic safety incidents, model-level factual error improvement, human capability transfer, general AGI safety, or consciousness detection.
- **Not achieved:** third-party validation, clinical or regulated use authorization, academic peer review, AI-system safety certification, or partnerships with major labs.
- Synthetic unit-test agreement is not a measure of real risk mitigation.

## Five pillars

1. **LUCEM** — source support, uncertainty, epistemic integrity.
2. **CENTRO** — external-action authorization, auditability, human checkpoints.
3. **Benchmark ℛ / EGRA** — falsifiable, comparable, logged evaluations.
4. **Prueba de separación renovativa** — whether people retain autonomous capability after removing AI assistance.
5. **ℛ-Labs / biolegitimidad** — normatively evaluate life, agency, plurality and reversibility while explicitly distinguishing moral reasoning from empirical inference.

## Research ethos

Verificar la evidencia. Contener la acción. Preservar la agencia.

Do not deploy the illustrative gate into production. An actual action-control gate requires independent authorization, complete tool coverage, sandboxing, security review, audit logs, incident handling and out-of-band approval. Do not use sensitive or production data in adverse testing.

The source documents and poetic expressions are works of philosophy and art, not evidence that AGI safety has been solved. Rights and licensing must be confirmed by the author before third-party reuse is advertised.
