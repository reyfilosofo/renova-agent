# Product family: ℛenova AI Safety / SERESARTE

**Four working product specifications; no sales, paid clients, certification or peer review asserted.**

- **LUCEM Evidence Integrity Audit:** source trace, claim annotation, unsupported citations and abstention QA. Prototype offline only; needs blind evaluators and externally verified sources.
- **CENTRO Action Authority Assessment:** sandbox exercises on scoped tool authorisation, interception completeness, action execution and audit. No live enforcement yet.
- **EGRA Evaluation Study:** preregistered comparison of LUCEM and CENTRO on controlled models/strong baselines; currently experimental.
- **Reversible Human Agency Study:** ethically reviewed adult learning evaluation of skills retained after removing AI assistance; planned, no participants enrolled.

Institutional governance implementation through **NOUS** must keep professional consulting separate from independent research and public-facing philosophical works.

**Not for sale/claim:** AGI Safety Guarantee, ℛ-Certified Safe AI, autonomous certification, consciousness detection via model conversation.

See local v0.4 pack for functional offline demonstrator, 14 tests, synthetic fixtures, fuller manuscript, product sheets, reference archives and financial/licensing gates.
