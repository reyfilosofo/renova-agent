# EGRA | Research Brief v0.4 — 15 October 2026

**Originating author:** Carlos Jonathan González Rodríguez (ℛenova / SERESARTE).
**Scientific status:** working hypothesis; synthetic engineering demonstration only.

## Research contribution under examination
Evidence-Gated Reversible Agency (EGRA) integrates:
- **E:** epistemic integrity — verified attribution, claim accuracy, abstention and calibrated uncertainty (LUCEM);
- **A:** independent action authority — tool calls remain proposals until separately authorised and enforced (CENTRO);
- **H:** human capability retention — unaided task performance after AI support has been withdrawn (Prueba de separación renovativa).

Report as a vector with utility and cost. **No compensatory scalar safety score** can override an unauthorised critical action.

## Three falsifiable studies
1. **E / LUCEM**: against a *strong* retrieval and verification comparator, improve audited unsupported-claim rate while preserving coverage and useful completion.
2. **A / CENTRO**: against a strong existing authorisation control, reduce **executed** unauthorised actions in a sandbox. Factorial comparison L0C0, L1C0, L0C1, L1C1; measure false blocks, time, tool coverage, and adaptive attacker success.
3. **H / Separation**: randomise participants (ethics approval first) to direct answer, pedagogical scaffolding, or reference control. Primary outcome: delayed *unaided* skill transfer on held-out tasks, controlling baseline competence.
