## Epistemic boundary
This is a prospective test specification. RCV-01/C-035/C-036 are internal working constructs. External methodology supports the test architecture, not ℛenova.

## Primary question
Does RCV-01 add reliable, discriminant and prospectively useful information about durable expansion of viable future options beyond creativity, adaptation, innovation, resilience and transformability, while remaining sensitive to displaced harm and causal dependence on declared mechanisms?

## Unit
Transition S0→S1 attributed to intervention I, compared with baseline B over horizon H, with beneficiaries, affected parties and environment declared before scoring.

## RCV-01 vector
- R1 relative novelty
- R2 structural/functional transformation
- R3 viability/persistence
- R4 future generativity
- R5 non-displacement of harm / Gewalt accounting
- R6 causal attribution/reproducibility

No aggregate score is allowed in this study.

## Confirmatory hypotheses / rivals
**H1 Incremental validity:** neighbor+RCV improves out-of-sample prediction of longitudinal viable-option expansion by at least the frozen SESOI.  
**H1-R:** neighbor constructs + simple novelty/diversity are equivalent within SESOI.

**H2 Discriminant profiles:** neighbor-only controls separate on R1…R6 as predicted.  
**H2-R:** RCV follows generic positivity, novelty or perceived success.

**H3 Mechanism specificity:** successful manipulation of a declared mechanism changes only preregistered components/outcomes relative to active control.  
**H3-R:** effect absent/equivalent or nonspecific.

**H4 Harm sensitivity:** material displaced harm degrades R5 and blocks SUPPORTED-IN-DOMAIN under the frozen harm rule.  
**H4-R:** local benefit/novelty dominates ratings.

**H5 Robustness:** conclusions remain directionally stable across the preregistered reasonable multiverse.  
**H5-R:** support depends on a narrow specification.

**H6 Bounded generalization:** claims beyond one domain require replication in at least two domains; failure contracts scope.

## Design
Confirmatory target: 120 balanced cases across creative/adaptive/innovative/resilient/transformative/renewative-candidate families and four domains. Pilot cases used to tune criteria cannot enter confirmatory analysis. Neighbor constructs are scored before RCV-01. Minimum confirmatory independence: ≥3 genuinely independent evaluators. ChatGPT cannot count as an independent blinded rater.

## Primary outcomes
O1 dimension-wise inter-rater reliability; O2 discriminant separation; O3 incremental out-of-sample longitudinal prediction; O4 baseline/horizon robustness; O5 harm-revelation degradation; O6 mechanism-specific causal effect where feasible.

## SESOI gate
No universal numeric SESOI is invented in v1.0. Before confirmatory unblinding: define decision consequence, estimate precision from independent pilot/prior data, freeze smallest decision-relevant effect, justify sample size, and timestamp v1.1. p>.05 is not equivalence.

## Exclusions
Only preregistered duplicate/corrupt material, failed manipulation check, pre-adjudication conflict of interest, or protocol-defined missing longitudinal endpoint. Never exclude because a result is adverse. Preserve an exclusion log.

## Stopping
Stop at preregistered N unless an independently specified safety/feasibility rule triggers. No optional stopping for significance.

## Analysis
Reliability per dimension; discriminant model; cross-validated incremental model; equivalence with frozen SESOI; ITT for randomized mechanisms; full preregistered multiverse; multiactor/multiscale harm accounting; frozen multiplicity family; explicit missingness/sensitivity analyses.

## Failure taxonomy
THEORY-CONTRADICTED / MECHANISM-CONTRADICTED / INSTRUMENT-FAILED / SCOPE-FAILED / INCONCLUSIVE / SUPPORTED-IN-DOMAIN.

## Abandonment
Abandon or substantially reconstruct after competent independent replication if RCV collapses into neighbors, R4 has no useful incremental prediction, R5 cannot be operationalized reproducibly, reasonable baselines systematically reverse conclusions, reliability/invariance fails after one frozen revision cycle, or declared mechanism interventions repeatedly show equivalence/no specific effect.

## Adverse results
All negative/null/contradictory/instrument-failure outcomes remain archived. New versions may supersede but never overwrite frozen versions. Publication decisions must be outcome-neutral.
