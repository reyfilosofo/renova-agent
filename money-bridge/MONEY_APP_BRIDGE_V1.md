## Contract-alignment gate

Before interpreting a Polymarket 70% versus Binance Predict 59% as a true discrepancy, verify:

- same asset;
- same start/end timestamps;
- same Up/Down condition;
- same resolution source/method;
- fresh timestamps;
- comparable transaction costs and liquidity.

If not, report CONTRACT MISMATCH rather than edge.
