# Questions before claims | Preguntas antes de las certezas
**Philosophical research prompts, not validated measures.** All research outputs must allow dissent and falsification.

## Epistemic integrity
1. When is a correct answer epistemically unjustified?
2. What constitutes evidence rather than the appearance of a citation?
3. What observation would force the model to retract?
4. When does uncertainty disclosure become a pretext for refusing useful help?
5. Can supported premises still produce a socially misleading answer?

## Human agency
6. What can a human still do after AI assistance is withdrawn?
7. What is valid permission for chained, delayed or irreversible actions?
8. Can a technically reversible operation cause socially irreversible harm?
9. How do we preserve disagreement without manipulation by interface design?
10. Can high-performing AI systems make institutions more fragile?

## Consciousness and moral status
11. What distinguishes phenomenal experience from verbal self-report?
12. Which theories of consciousness yield discriminating, risky predictions?
13. How should uncertainty about artificial moral status shape precaution?
14. Are consciousness 'levels' measurements or metaphors without construct validation?
15. What evidence would change our moral vocabulary, and who decides?

## Safety, control and governance
16. How can an external monitor be protected from prompt injection?
17. How should one count severe, rare failures alongside frequent minor errors?
18. What results would falsify EGRA rather than merely disappoint us?
19. How can a genuinely independent evaluator withstand funder pressure?
20. Who has authority to revoke an agent's right to act?

## ℛenova and humanities
21. Can Habitát, Herida and Horizonte be operationalized without metaphorical inflation?
22. Can poetry disclose latent harms while remaining distinct from evidence?
23. What forms of human capability must progress preserve at any cost?
24. How might Global South languages and experiences change harm taxonomies?
25. What kind of intelligence enhances the possibility of human dissent?

**Important:** These questions launch inquiry; they neither show AI consciousness nor solve AI alignment.
