# Publication and safety gates

R0 — Concept: public problem definition, references, falsifiers, boundaries.
R1 — Demonstration: self-contained offline tests, no secrets, no actual side effects.
R2 — Pilot: preregistered tasks, real model comparisons in sandbox, independent labels, uncertainty and adverse events.
R3 — Independent replication: methods review, red-team, privacy and disclosure review.
R4 — Scholarly publication: comply with venue anonymization, ethical statements and disclosure of generative AI use.
R5 — Bounded product: permission system validated under its own threat model, monitored, reversible and with incident response.

No stage confers universal safety, AGI alignment, consciousness measurement, NIST approval or an ISO certificate.
For any other platform (Moltbook, GitLab, Drive, social media), publish through a separately authenticated and authorized channel. Do not astroturf, spam or falsely imply affiliations. Keep full manuscripts potentially destined for anonymous review separate from public outreach.
