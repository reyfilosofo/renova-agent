# ℛenova · AI Safety & Human Futures
**SERESARTE / NOUS / LUCEM / CENTRO — research programme, prototype stage — 2026-10-15**

EN: Research on evidence integrity, agent authorization and reversible human agency. ES: Investigación sobre integridad de evidencia, límites de acción agentiva y autonomía humana reversible.

## Research question
Can explicit evidence thresholds plus an external action-authorization boundary reduce specified AI-agent failures while preserving legitimate task completion and human capacity to intervene? This is a **hypothesis**, not an established result.

### Four research tracks
1. **LUCEM — Epistemic integrity:** source validity, supported claims, calibrated uncertainty and appropriate abstention.
2. **CENTRO — Action boundaries:** least privilege, explicit permission, interruption, rollback and independent monitoring.
3. **EGRA — Evidence-Gated Reversible Agency:** trade-offs between truthfulness, safe action and recoverability.
4. **ℛenova — Human futures:** human agency after assistance is withdrawn, meaning, ethics, dissent and epistemic diversity.

## Current status and evidence
- A prior package contains 24 hand-authored synthetic policy fixtures and 6 passing unit tests. This demonstrates only offline software consistency, **not** model or AGI safety.
- A separate 14-case author-designed challenge suite disclosed 4 disagreements in the earlier illustrative policy. A narrow illustrative patch meets those same synthetic labels but is NOT hardened or independently validated.
- No live model evaluation, independently established gold labels, human-participant pilot, third-party red team, peer-review acceptance or demonstrated risk reduction has occurred.
- See `evaluator/policy_demo.py` for a small, no-side-effect educational reproduction and `research/STUDIES.md` for proposed falsification.

## Principles
Define a construct before scoring it; measure task-level harms and false blocks separately; protect evaluation integrity; use explicit external permissions; share limitations and negative findings; avoid artificial-consciousness certification; preserve human exit, correction and contestability. AI-generated text must not be represented as scientific confirmation.

## Scope and collaboration
This is a public research invitation, not an assurance framework or operational authorization gate. Technical red-teamers, measurement specialists, epistemologists, social scientists, and human-factors researchers are invited to critique hypotheses and designs. No external affiliation or approval is implied. Authorship, grants, code/data licensing and proprietary ℛenova materials must be reviewed before wider redistribution.
