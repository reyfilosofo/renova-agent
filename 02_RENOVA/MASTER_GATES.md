# ℛenova — Master Promotion Gates

Updated: 2026-09-08

A filename containing `FINAL`, `MASTER`, `MAESTRO`, `DEFINITIVO` or a higher version number is not sufficient to establish canonical authority.

## Promotion states

`DISCOVERED → VERIFIED_SOURCE → MASTER_CANDIDATE → MASTER`

`COMMERCIAL_ACTIVE` and `SUPERSEDED` are operational states and do not by themselves establish editorial or intellectual master status.

## Gate 1 — Identity and ontology
- Canonical work/series ID resolved against ARCHIVO TOTAL CJGR.
- Work, edition, version, compilation and derivative distinguished.
- Relationship to adjacent ℛenova works explicitly classified or left `UNKNOWN_RELATION`.
- No new ID minted while an existing ID may apply.

## Gate 2 — File integrity and lineage
- Exact source file identified.
- SHA-256/checksum registered privately.
- Duplicate/near-duplicate candidates compared.
- Predecessor/successor relation established where evidence supports it.
- Rollback source retained.

## Gate 3 — Intellectual and scientific integrity
For empirical, scientific, mathematical or consciousness-related claims:
- proposition classified as established finding, model, hypothesis, inference, metaphor or authorial thesis;
- relevant evidence and references audited;
- mathematical definitions, dimensions, assumptions and derivations checked where applicable;
- unsupported empirical certainty removed;
- domain-expert review required when the text makes consequential technical claims.

## Gate 4 — Bibliography and citation
- References exist and are traceable.
- Quotation/translations have attributable sources where required.
- DOI/edition/page metadata checked where relevant.
- No fabricated source, citation or bibliographic identifier.

## Gate 5 — Editorial
- Structural edit complete.
- Style/orthotypography complete.
- Repetition, contradiction and terminology drift controlled.
- Contents/index match final pagination.
- Editable source or reproducible production source identified.

## Gate 6 — Production/preflight
- Trim size, bleed, margins and fonts checked.
- Images meet intended production quality.
- Cover/spine generated from actual final pagination and stock.
- PDF preflight passes.
- Physical/digital proof reviewed where applicable.

## Gate 7 — Legal/IP
- Authorship/ownership status documented.
- Third-party images, translations and excerpts reviewed.
- ISBN is real when required; never invented.
- Privacy/confidential information removed from public editions.

## Gate 8 — Commercial readiness
- Product/edition identity is explicit.
- Stripe/payment configuration, price and delivery artifact match the edition.
- Commercial source is not silently promoted to intellectual master.
- Delivery workflow has rollback and deduplication controls.

## Gate 9 — Final approval
A work can be `MASTER` only when:
- blockers = 0;
- major issues ≤ 2 and explicitly accepted or resolved;
- global score ≥ 93 when a scored audit is used;
- source, version, date and decision are logged;
- an explicit canonical promotion decision exists.

## Current corpus rule
No global ℛenova master is currently inferred from the existence of H V7.5, OMNIA QUALIA, Filosofía Renovativa, Ciencia de la Vida or any later-named compilation. Until textual relationship evidence is produced, adjacent works remain independent or `UNKNOWN_RELATION` rather than being merged automatically.
