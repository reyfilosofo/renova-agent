# Ω PREDICT · SOURCE REGISTRY

## Level A — Primary
Official bodies and regulators; governments and courts; companies and investor relations; Coinbase and direct exchange data; blockchain protocols and verified explorers; sports leagues and teams; official statistics; contractual market rules; market resolution/oracle sources; direct exchange order-book data.

## Level B — Professional
Major news agencies; established financial media; specialist journalists; professional statistics providers; academic publications; recognized researchers; specialized market-data services.

## Level C — Social signals
X, Reddit, public Discord, public Telegram, crypto communities, forums, independent traders and analysts.

Use Level C primarily for discovery and early-warning signals. Do not automatically treat it as confirmation.

## Level D — Rumors
Treat only as investigation leads until independently corroborated.

## Source review fields
`source_name`, `source_type`, `source_level`, `url`, `publisher`, `published_at`, `event_occurred_at`, `retrieved_at`, `timezone`, `primary_or_secondary`, `direct_quote_or_paraphrase`, `corroborated_by`, `conflicts_with`, `financial_incentive`, `known_bias_or_limitation`, `reliability_notes`, `status`.

## X/social protocol
1. Prefer the original account/post.
2. Verify timestamp.
3. Determine whether the post preceded or followed the price move.
4. Seek independent corroboration.
5. Inspect relevant replies and corrections.
6. Separate bots/spam/coordinated behavior from organic evidence where possible.
7. Identify incentives.
8. Search for contrary evidence.
9. Ask whether the information is already priced.

**Principle:** freshness and causal timing matter. A true fact that was public before a price move may have little residual informational value after incorporation.
