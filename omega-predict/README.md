## Canonical model
**GitHub** is the version-controlled canonical text layer.  
**Drive** is the operational/editorial workspace and live ledger.

## Operating principle
The system does not exist to maximize trades. It exists to improve the quality, auditability, and calibration of decisions. When evidence is insufficient, abstention is a valid output.

## Human authority
The human operator retains final authority over bets, purchases, sales, transfers, positions, and any financial execution. Ω PREDICT output is intelligence input, not automatic transaction authorization.
